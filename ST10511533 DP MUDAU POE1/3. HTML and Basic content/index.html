Welcome to Sweet Crumbs Bakery

Welcome to Sweet Crumbs Bakery, your local bakery for fresh, delicious and affordable baked products.

Sweet Crumbs Bakery was established in 2021 by a local baker who wanted to provide fresh and affordable baked products to the surrounding community.

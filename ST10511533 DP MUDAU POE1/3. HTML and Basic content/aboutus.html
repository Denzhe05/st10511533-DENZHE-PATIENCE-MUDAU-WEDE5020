About Us


Our History

Sweet Crumbs Bakery started as a small home-based bakery in 2021. The business later expanded into a physical shop and continues to provide quality baked products to the local community.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide customers with fresh, high-quality and affordable baked products while offering friendly and reliable customer service.

Our Vision

Our vision is to become a trusted local bakery known for quality products, creative cake designs and excellent customer service.
